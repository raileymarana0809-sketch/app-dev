# Aftersun (2022)

> A memory of a father and daughter's holiday, pieced together years later.

## About the Film

**Aftersun** is a drama directed by **Charlotte Wells** in her feature debut. It follows *Sophie*, who looks back on a summer holiday at a Turkish resort with her father, *Calum*, when she was eleven.

## Why It's My Favorite

- Quiet, emotional storytelling
- Great performances from the leads
- Memory is used in a creative way
- The soundtrack fits every scene
- I am all for movies that tackle vulnerability and mental health

## Things that will always stick with me

1. The camcorder footage mixes past and present
2. Small moments and quiet scenes say more than long dialogue
3. The ending stays with you
